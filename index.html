01 / PROFILE

About me.

An interdisciplinary researcher at the intersection of AI and imaging.

Research profile

I am a deep learning and computer vision researcher with an academic background in ICT and computer science. My work has focused on predicting image noise levels using convolutional neural networks, as well as applying object detection and semantic segmentation to automated image assessment.

I earned my M.Sc. (Engg.) in ICT from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) and my B.Sc. (Engg.) in CSE from Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University (MBSTU).

My experience includes research at the Ferdinand-Braun-Institut in Berlin, university teaching and industry software development.
